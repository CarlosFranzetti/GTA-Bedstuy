## Physics notes for the PHYSICS lane (what I did see)

- **Car backed against a wall (camera):** three cases at (-8.3, 9.4), (-8.3, 10.0), (-8.3, 11.7), car heading +90 degrees, a static wall about 2.7 m behind the car centre (rear bumper about 0.35 m from the wall), placed with `teleport`. The car stayed at rest (speed 0.00, no jitter). The camera sat 2.34 m behind the centre (about at the rear bumper) at height 2.33 m, i.e. above the roof, never inside the car or the wall (`raycastStatic` from car to camera: no hit; camera point not inside a static). PASS, but the view is a roof-top shot (wall0.jpg).
- **Teleport does not check overlap:** `__gtaDebug.teleport` puts a car or the player at any x,z, including 2.7 m from a wall or on top of a pickup. Fine for tests, so the PHYSICS lane should not trust it.
- **Pistol areaway (defect 2):** a pickup inside the collision of a fence or railing. Sweep all 65 pickup defs (51 bottle, 10 brick, 4 pistol) for foot reachability from spawn.
- **Ped/ped overlap (defect 4).**
- **Knock-down:** peds hit by thrown objects or shots go `down` at once, then fleeing 4 s, then `walking` again in the one timeline I recorded. A thrown brick leaves the count 1 -> 0 and the player falls back to fists as designed.
- Player entering a car with F from 1.8 m worked (job A3, "in car: true"), car stayed put while the player looked around.
- Not looked at: car vs car, car vs ped, wall bounce, sliding, handling feel. Jobs D and E never ran past boot.

## Works

- Title: none of 16 keys (W A S D F E M P Tab Shift Q G Ctrl Backspace X ArrowUp), 3 left clicks or a right click starts the game. The player does not move behind the title (0.000 m with W held 1.5 s of simulation, and 0.00 m in 1.5 s of real time in job A). No game HUD is visible over the title (`body.title-up`, no visible HUD children). Space starts the game (jobs A3, B, C). Controls and Credits are present.
- Camera after look: view held to 2.0 s, then a slow start and faster return. On foot, 400 px look, yaw 1.062 stays to t=2.25 s, then 1.052 (2.5 s), 0.992, 0.833, 0.600, 0.378, ... 0.000 by 6 s. In a car, 450 px look, the yaw holds to t=2.25 s, then swings through about 180 degrees to behind the car by t=5 s.
- Action button: `G` and left mouse (pointer-locked) work. `Q` cycles in order bottle > brick > pistol > fists. Bottle throw (count 2 -> 1) and brick throw (1 -> 0, falls back to fists), pistol fire (12 -> 11), the HUD widget top right ("12/24"), and left mouse in a car does nothing. Pickups placed (65: 51 bottles, 10 bricks, 4 pistols). Pistol, bottle and brick pickups work when stood on. Wanted stars rise (0 -> 1 -> 2 -> 3 after downing peds and firing). Stagger -> flee -> walking again seen.
- People: upright posture, straight trouser legs, shoes sit on the pavement and are visible from a 1.7 m low camera (ped_shoes1, ped_shoes2), varied faces and clothes (ped_side1, ped_front frames).
- Streets: bluestone sidewalk slabs, street trees, overhead wires (chase_spawn_calm.jpg), brick buildings, chain-link fence with plants (ped_front.jpg).

## Not tested (and why)

Jobs D and E (walk/sprint/jump, entering and driving several car types, brake and reverse, radio, phone, map, first story mission, a side mission, pause menu and volume sliders, saving and loading with hands, day/night, rain) crashed with "Target crashed" (cgroup OOM) before or right after the game started, and I was told to stop running jobs. Also untested: Enter, gamepad A/Start and touch tap on the title, the five loading posters (not in the build), engine sound levels (no audio capture), shop interiors, and sprint/jump feel. Under D and E, expect nothing from this report either way.
