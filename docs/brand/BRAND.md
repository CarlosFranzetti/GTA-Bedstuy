# GTA Bed-Stuy: brand one-pager

## The idea
The mark is a brownstone: a cornice, tall arched windows and the steep front stoop that every Bed-Stuy block is built
around. It is drawn as one gold facade with a white stoop climbing to the parlour door, so it works as a square icon at 32 px
and beside the wordmark. The wordmark keeps the title screen's order: a small, wide-spaced "GTA" in gold over a large
"BED-STUY" in white, with a gold rule.

## Colours
| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#0a0910` | backgrounds, the one-colour dark version |
| Gold | `#f2c230` | facade, cornice, "GTA", the rule |
| White | `#ffffff` | stoop, "BED-STUY", the one-colour light version |

Full colour is for dark backgrounds only. On light or busy backgrounds use the one-colour ink or white version.

## Fonts
Anton (display, "GTA" and "BED-STUY") and Barlow Condensed (text). Both are SIL Open Font License 1.1, shipped in
`public/fonts/` with their licences (`OFL-Anton.txt`, `OFL-Barlow.txt`). In the logo files the letters are outlines, so no
font is needed to view them. Do not set the wordmark in any other face.

## Clear space and size
- Clear space on every side: one fifth of the mark's width (about the width of the door). Nothing enters it.
- Minimum size: mark 24 px (16 px as a favicon only, where it reads as a silhouette), lockup 120 px wide, stacked 96 px wide.
- Keep the proportions. Do not stretch, tilt, outline, add shadows, or recolour outside the three colours above.
- Keep the gap between the white stoop and the gold facade; do not merge them.

## What not to do
- Never imitate the Grand Theft Auto wordmark or typeface: no Pricedown or lookalike, no stacked heavy italic slab, no long-tailed letter.
- Never imitate the Rockstar "R with a star in a rounded square", and never put the mark in a rounded-square badge.
- Never use any other company's mark, or a real person's likeness.
- Never add lettering to the mark. "GTA Bed-Stuy" is a free, non-commercial fan tribute. Where space allows, say
  "A fan tribute. Not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two."
