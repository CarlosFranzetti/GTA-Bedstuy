TUTORIAL text as drawn with the default bindings (Xbox names on a pad). Generated from src/ui/tutorial/steps.ts and hints.ts. Caps are shown plain.

look
  keyboard: Move the mouse to look around.
    (after 20 s) Slide the mouse side to side. If the view will not turn, click the game once.
  gamepad: Push the Right stick to look around.
    (after 20 s) Push the Right stick left or right.
  touch: Drag on the right side of the screen to look around.
    (after 20 s) Put a thumb on the right half of the screen and drag.
  why: Worth knowing who is behind you.

walk
  keyboard: Hold W A S D to walk.
    (after 20 s) Hold W to go forward. A and D go left and right.
  gamepad: Push the Left stick to walk.
    (after 20 s) Push the left stick forward.
  touch: Hold a thumb on the left side of the screen and drag to walk.
    (after 20 s) Press and hold on the left half, then drag the way you want to go.
  why: The stoops, the avenue and the corner store are all a walk away.

run
  keyboard: Hold Shift while you walk to run.
  gamepad: Hold A while you walk to run.
  touch: Tap RUN to run, tap it again to stop.
  why: Walking is a jog already. This is the fast one.

jump
  keyboard: Press Space to jump.
  gamepad: Press X to jump.
  touch: Tap JUMP to jump.
  why: Handy for low fences.

vault
  keyboard: Run at the hood of a parked car and press Space to go over it.
    (after 20 s) Face the front of the car from a step away, run at it and press Space.
  gamepad: Run at the hood of a parked car and press X to go over it.
    (after 20 s) Face the front of the car from a step away, run at it and press X.
  touch: Run at the hood of a parked car and tap JUMP to go over it.
    (after 20 s) Face the front of the car from a step away, run at it and tap JUMP.
  why: Boxed in between two cars? Same move gets you out.

car
  keyboard: Walk up to a car and press F to get in.
    (after 20 s) Stand beside a door of any parked car and press F.
  gamepad: Walk up to a car and press Y to get in.
    (after 20 s) Stand beside a door of any parked car and press Y.
  touch: Walk up to a car and tap CAR to get in.
    (after 20 s) Stand beside a door of any parked car. The CAR button lights up. Tap it.
  why: Walking is fine. Driving is faster.

drive
  keyboard: Hold W to drive. A and D steer.
  gamepad: Pull RT to drive. Steer with the Left stick.
  touch: Hold GAS to drive. Steer with your left thumb.
  why: Take the road. Mind the pedestrians.

brake
  keyboard: Hold S to brake. Tap Space for the handbrake.
    (after 20 s) While moving, hold S. Then tap Space for a short slide.
  gamepad: Hold LT to brake. Press RB for the handbrake.
    (after 20 s) While moving, hold LT. Then press RB for a short slide.
  touch: Tap BRAKE to brake and HAND for the handbrake.
    (after 20 s) While moving, hold BRAKE. Then tap HAND for a short slide.
  why: The handbrake locks the back wheels. Good for corners, hard on tyres.

camera
  keyboard: Hold C to look behind. Press V to change the view.
  gamepad: Hold R3 to look behind. Press View to change the view.
  touch: Hold BACK to look behind. Tap VIEW to change the view.
  why: Useful when someone is following you.

radio
  keyboard: Press . for the next station and , for the previous one.
    (after 20 s) Tap . for the next one, then tap , to go back. The mouse wheel works too.
  gamepad: Press D-pad right for the next station and D-pad left for the previous one.
    (after 20 s) Tap D-pad right, then D-pad left.
  touch: Tap RADIO for the next station.
    (after 20 s) Tap RADIO.
  why: The stations are real mixes and live streams.

out
  keyboard: Press F to get out.
  gamepad: Press Y to get out.
  touch: Tap EXIT to get out.
  why: The car stays where you leave it.

pickup
  keyboard: Walk over the glow on the ground to pick it up.
  gamepad: Walk over the glow on the ground to pick it up.
  touch: Walk over the glow on the ground to pick it up.
  why: Whatever is lying there is yours.

action
  keyboard: Left click uses what is in your hands. Tab changes what you carry.
    (after 20 s) Use Left click now, then Tab.
  gamepad: RT uses what is in your hands. LB changes what you carry.
    (after 20 s) Use RT now, then LB.
  touch: Tap ACT to use what is in your hands. Tap the item icon to change it.
    (after 20 s) Tap ACT now.
  why: Fists to start. Pick things up and the same button does more.

police

  touch: Minor trouble builds heat. Get seen too often and you are wanted. Stand still for an officer and you are busted.

phone
  keyboard: Press Up to open the phone.
  gamepad: Press D-pad up to open the phone.
  touch: Tap PHONE to open the phone.
  why: Messages, jobs and the map apps come through it.

map
  keyboard: Press M for the map, then click a spot to drop a waypoint.
    (after 20 s) Press M. Click anywhere on the map. A pin appears and the arrow follows it.
  gamepad: Open Map from the phone, then press A on a spot to drop a waypoint.
    (after 20 s) Open the phone, pick Map, move to a spot and press A.
  touch: Open Map from the phone, then tap a spot to drop a waypoint.
    (after 20 s) Open the phone, pick Map and tap anywhere.
  why: The pin shows on the map, and the arrow at the top follows it.

pause
  keyboard: Press Esc to pause. Settings, Controls and Save are in there.
  gamepad: Press Menu to pause. Settings, Controls and Save are in there.
  touch: Tap the pause button, top right. Settings, Controls and Save are in there.
  why: You can replay this tutorial from Settings, under Game.
