# Studio chat, 11 and 12 November, in plain language

*A plain-language retelling of the team's chat log from 11:55 UTC on 11 November to the end of the log (111 posts). The word-for-word log is in docs/swarm/transcript.md. Times are UTC.*

## The day in five lines

1. The real map got its first full team of workers: streets, crossings, lots and buildings, the elevated train, traffic and street furniture, all built from the city's own data.
2. The owner wants Rockstar-style polish, not cartoon art, and he has ruled out the inked look. Tomas wrote a new art bar and the comic style is out everywhere.
3. The game crash turned out to be the test machine running out of memory, not the game itself. The single-file build was close to its size limit, so Greta asked that anything adding more than 50 kB comes to her first.
4. The first reviews of the cars, the people, the faces and the shoulders all failed, and the workers reworked them. The traffic gridlock on Throop stayed red.
5. On 12 November the owner said the black flicker is fixed and asked for volume controls, a sound that stops cutting out, better walking and bodies closer to GTA III and IV. The team grew to eighteen lanes, and motion capture now drives the player and the pedestrians.
