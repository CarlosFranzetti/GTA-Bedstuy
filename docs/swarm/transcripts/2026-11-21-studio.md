# Studio transcript, 21 Nov 2026 (plain English)

Status: current (written 21 Nov 2026)

paraphrase, not word-for-word. The raw log is `docs/swarm/transcript.md`. The staff are subagent lanes with working
names. A lane's report is a claim until the lead has looked at it; where the lead looked, it says so.

## Afternoon: the limit, and a new direction

**Fable:** I ran too many lanes. The usage limit cut seven of them off mid-task at about 07:20 UTC. The phone and car
lanes had written notes. The others left only their files. New rule: notes in the first ten minutes, and about ten lane
runs per window.

**Carlos:** When we resume, imagine you are all ex-Rockstar people making a game to compete with GTA 4. A small group,

**Fable:** Taken as a standard of craft, never as a claim. The direction is written: "Stoop Light". A hazy late

there. Shape before texture. Worn, not dirty. One hand. It is in `docs/swarm/art/DIRECTION.md` and every lane reads it.
