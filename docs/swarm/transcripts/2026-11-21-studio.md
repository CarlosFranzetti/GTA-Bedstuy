Status: current (written 21 Nov 2026)

This is a readable retelling of the day so far, written by the lead from the lanes' own posts and reports. It is
paraphrase, not word-for-word. The raw log is `docs/swarm/transcript.md`. The staff are subagent lanes with working
names. A lane's report is a claim until the lead has looked at it; where the lead looked, it says so.

## The first wave

**Fable:** I looked at it. Gold brownstone, white stoop, "GTA" small over "BED-STUY" large. It looks like neither the
GTA wordmark nor the Rockstar badge. Sent to Carlos.

**Ines (study):** The GTA 4 and 5 study is done. Twenty rules at the top. The main ones: silhouette first, build crowds
from parts instead of whole bodies, faces get the budget, clothing is shape before texture, and a car's class should
read from its outline.

**Fable:** How solid is it?

**Ines:** Sixteen pages opened, four read in full. No published triangle budgets for people, no handling table per
class. Those numbers are marked as estimates. Treat them as starting points.

**Edie (docs):** The README is rewritten from the code, with the new header. Eighty-six markdown files now say whether
they are current, partly stale or history. I found two things for you: there is no licence file, and the desktop apps
build on GitHub but nobody has published a release.

**Fable:** Noted for Carlos. I also removed AI model names from the old planning docs and fixed a help line that still
said Left Ctrl.

## Walls, cars and people

**Tamsin (textures):** Seven kinds of wall now draw as one material. Building draw calls at the home view dropped from
104 to 54 on the low tier. The low tier is still 3 over its budget there.

**Fable:** I looked at your frames. Walls are fine. The road is a flat black sheet and it fills half the picture. That
is the next job. A new lane takes streets, sidewalks and shopfronts.

**Marlowe (vehicles):** Handling is rebuilt for the car you drive. Cars lean, dive and squat. Grip lets go gradually and
comes back. Fifteen classes, so a hatchback and a bus feel different. Traffic cars are untouched, with one switch to
change that later. I did not get to the car bodies.

**Rowan (bugs):** Marlowe, your change turns eight driving tests red. With the keyboard the car now turns 32 to 54
degrees where the test wants more than 80. The handbrake drift is weaker, and in the chase test the fleeing car rams the
police. The two five-minute traffic tests are green.

**Fable:** That matters. A car that will not turn enough on the keyboard is a worse game, whatever the model says. It
goes back for a retune before anything ships.

**Fable:** I looked at the sheet of all 37 cars. They share one wedge nose and one wheel. A new lane is giving each class
its own outline, then real lamps, grilles and glass.

**Marlo (people):** Beards used to cover eyes and lips. They sit on the jaw now. I added wardrobe entries. I could not
fix the hairline or find why some collars are orange.

**Fable:** I looked. Faces are acceptable. Clothes are the problem: a box around the hips, box cuffs, square shoulders,
a flap under the belt. A repair lane is on exactly those, one at a time, each proven in a picture.

**Odalys (landmarks):** Twenty places are built under invented names: parks, housing blocks, churches, an armoury,
schools, a hospital, a library, a station house, a firehouse. Tests pass. The in-game pictures are queued.

**Fable:** Not seen by me yet. No names go to Carlos until I have checked them.
