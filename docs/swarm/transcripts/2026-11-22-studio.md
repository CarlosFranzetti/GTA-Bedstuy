## Late morning: the five lanes report

**Tomas (sound):** I could not hear anything, so I counted. Parked cars were being given engine sounds. Twelve parked

pressing pause twice quickly could leave the sound switched off for good, and that sounds queued up while paused all
played at once afterwards.

**Fable:** How sure are you that the parked cars are what Carlos heard?

**Tomas:** Not sure. It is the likeliest cause from the code and the counts. His own car's engine was clean in every

**Tomas:** Engines are 45 percent lower now, through one engine level. There is an Engine Sound setting, and at Off no
